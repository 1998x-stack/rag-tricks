# Evidential Geometry

Knowledge becomes credible through visible relationships. A field of separate marks converges into a small set of structured paths, then into one deliberate outcome. The composition expresses selection and accountability without illustrating a literal machine.

Deep blue provides a stable ground. Muted jade marks the continuity of evidence, while a restrained copper accent identifies the moment of judgment. The palette is carefully calibrated so that hierarchy survives both a large cover and a small library thumbnail.

Typography is architectural: an oversized Latin title anchors the upper field, with Chinese serif text supplying an editorial counterweight. Labels stay sparse. Baselines, margins and optical spacing receive the painstaking attention of an expert book designer.

The lower field uses repeated geometric observations whose connections become more selective toward the right. Each line and node has a structural role; refinement comes from alignment and negative space, not ornament. The intended finish is meticulously crafted, quiet and precise.

This visual language treats uncertainty as part of knowledge. Some marks remain separate, resisting the suggestion that all information has already been proved. The work should communicate the judgment and care of a professionally edited technical publication.
